Music, tuned to your ears.

Monochrome is an open-source, privacy-focused music player with Hi-Res playback, a full equalizer studio, and headphone correction built in. It runs in your browser, on Android, and on desktop.

Draw the sound you want.

The EQ Studio shapes playback with graphic and parametric filters, AutoEQ headphone correction, and microphone-measured room correction. This demo uses the same kinds of filters.

Drag a node, or focus one and use the arrow keys.

Demo only. It never touches your own music. The noise is quiet, but lower your volume before you press play.

Graphic EQ
From 3 to 32 bands with a configurable frequency range, plus presets like Rock, Jazz, and Classical.
Parametric EQ
Peaking, low shelf, and high shelf filters with frequency, gain, and Q. Set a band to Mid or Side to shape only the center or the stereo width.
AutoEQ
Pick your headphone model and a target curve such as Harman, then generate correction filters you can still adjust by hand.
Speaker EQ
Measure each speaker with a microphone and generate room correction for 2.0, 5.1, or 7.1 setups. Import and export EqualizerAPO files.

Everything around the sound.

Quality, library, scrobbling, visuals, and themes. The details that listeners and tinkerers ask for, in one player.

Playback quality
Choose Hi-Res Lossless (24-bit), Lossless (16-bit), High, Low, or Auto. Ask for Dolby Atmos when a track offers it, and render it natively on Safari or Edge.
Between tracks
Gapless playback, crossfade from 1 to 12 seconds, silence removal, and ReplayGain by track or album.
Spatial and mono
Crossfeed for headphones, HRTF virtual-speaker presets for multichannel audio, adjustable stereo width, and a mono mode.
Your library
Playlists and folders. Import from CSV (including Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music exports), JSPF, XSPF, XML, or M3U, and export as CSV or JSON. Play local files from a folder on your device in supported browsers.
Scrobbling
Last.fm, Libre.fm, ListenBrainz, and Maloja, with a scrobble threshold you set.
Visualizers
Full-screen LCD Pixels, Particles, Unknown Pleasures, Butterchurn (Milkdrop), and Kawarp, with a photosensitivity warning and brightness and sensitivity controls.
Themes and type
Black, White, Dark, Ocean, Purple, Forest, Mocha, Macchiato, Frappé, and Latte, plus custom CSS and a community theme store. Pick a Google Font or upload your own.
Everyday tools
Speed from 0.01x to 100x with pitch preservation, a sleep timer, lyrics, a queue, and keyboard shortcuts you can rebind.

Questions.

Do I need an account?

An account is for syncing your library between devices. You can sign in with Google, GitHub, Discord, or email when you want sync.

Does Monochrome host music?

No. Monochrome is an independent client. It doesn't host, store, or distribute media files, and it isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any music streaming service. Content comes from third-party services and APIs that Monochrome doesn't control, and you're responsible for using it lawfully where you live.

Can I bring my playlists with me?

Yes. Import from CSV, including exports from Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music, or from JSPF, XSPF, XML, and M3U files. Imports can miss tracks, so Monochrome lists anything it couldn't add, and you can export that list.

Is there an iPhone app?

There's an iOS build, but it has to be sideloaded and the project doesn't support the sideloading process. The download page links a guide.

Can I host my own sync?

Yes. Monochrome can use your own PocketBase database and auth instance. The Docker guide on GitHub covers setup.

Where do I ask for help?

Join the Discord, or look through the project on GitHub.
